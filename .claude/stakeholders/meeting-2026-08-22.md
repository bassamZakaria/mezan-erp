# Stakeholder review — 2026-08-22

Nine stakeholders (end-user, designer, product, owner, implementation-consultant,
support-agent, accountant, sales-manager, warehouse-keeper) reviewed the Phase-1
desktop slice independently against the same 10 screenshots, plus Zemam's public
marketing pages. Published minutes:
https://claude.ai/code/artifact/af4ca89b-cd49-45b9-bd65-503db35e4963

## Verdict

Demoable on one path — login, dashboard, customers, suppliers, chart of accounts,
branches, warehouses, settings. Breaks at "make me an invoice". Demo the setup and
control story, not the transaction story, until the five findings below close.

## Consensus findings

1. `SalesInvoicesScreen.tsx:93` — فاتورة جديدة is a primary button with no
   `onClick` and no قريباً tag. Looks live, does nothing.
2. `sample-data.ts:39-41` — مراكز التكلفة / القيود المحاسبية / الفترات المالية are
   clickable, untagged, and dead. Named by the owner as the demo-killer.
3. `packages/domain/src/post-journal-entry.ts:41-70` enforces balance with a typed
   `UnbalancedEntryError` — and nothing calls it. No document posts an entry, no
   screen shows one.
4. `CustomersScreen.tsx:123` — متبقي من الحد is credit limit minus the *static*
   opening balance, ignoring unpaid invoices. Stale credit number, and it lives on
   the customer card rather than at the point of sale. Catalog requires it in the
   list (`01-sales.md:29`), so this is an in-phase defect.
5. Invoice rows open nothing while customer rows open a detail panel. The
   `DocumentDetail` primitive is built and unused; `CustomersScreen.tsx:390` has the
   working pattern. Row-action contract belongs in `DocumentList` before Phase 3 or
   it becomes a ~40-list retrofit.

(honest tags, live remaining credit, row-action contract, truthful licence chips)
and make the pre-confirm journal preview (الأثر المحاسبي) the first thing Phase 2
ships. Reorders nothing.
