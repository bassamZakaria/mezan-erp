Decision 2 · how far the chrome sits from the window edge

Today the header runs 0 → 2560 with padding-inline: 6px, while page content is inset 24px. The bar touches the glass and the content does not, so the chrome reads as the browser's, not the product's. Three ways to move it "a little bit inside".

Decision 3 · which row is taller

Row one — brand, search, scope, the user — is 44px. Row two, the workspace's screens, is 54px. The secondary row is 10px taller than the primary one, which is why the eye lands on the chips first. Two ways to put that back the right way up.

Decision 4 · the settings form, as the worst case

On a 2560 viewport every label sits at one inline edge and its control at the other — "تفعيل تعدد العملات" and its toggle are 1,400px apart. The commit button «حفظ الإعدادات» is grey-on-cream at the far corner and reads as disabled, while nine filled navy currency chips carry more weight than it does. Two ways to fix the density.
