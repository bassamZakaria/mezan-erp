Mezan in Cyag Blue

Mezan’s brand and accent families re-cut from the cyag.net palette, rendered on the real sales-invoice shell so the swap can be judged as a screen rather than as a list of hex values. Nothing in packages/ui/src/tokens/tokens.css has been changed.

Palette read from cyag.net/en — three _next stylesheets, September 2026.

What Cyag ships

The source palette

Cyag runs on one gradient and a slate neutral ramp. Only the first three values are brand; the rest is Tailwind’s slate scale, which Mezan already answers with its own warm-paper ramp.

The constraint that decides the mapping

#0099F3 cannot carry white text

3.07

White on Cyag’s primary fails WCAG AA (needs 4.5)

Cyag gets away with it because its primary button is a gradient — the label sits mostly over the dark #0000D5 end. Mezan fills flat: buttons, active chips, table selection, the KPI figure. So the bright blue becomes the accent family (rails, focus, eyebrows, the mark), and the deep blue becomes brand. That is the same division of labour teal and gold already have.

Flat blue, or Cyag’s gradient

Specimen 3 — every sidebar, in both themes

Seven rails, twice

The sidebar is its own token family (--chrome*): it holds one colour in both themes and never inherits the surface or ink ramp, which is why it can be swapped whole without touching anything else. Both rows below are pinned — the top row renders light and the bottom dark no matter which theme you are reading in, so the toggle above changes only the page around them. Ratios are the rail ink and the active marker against the rail itself; every one clears its bar.

The same seven, in the screen

How each rail sits against the work

A rail is judged next to the data, not on its own, and it has to hold in both themes — Mezan ships the toggle. Each option is shown light on the left and dark on the right, pinned the same way. Warm paper, white cards, the ink ramp and the semantic pills are identical throughout; the only variable is the chrome family.

The exact edit to tokens.css

Nine declarations in the light :root, and the same nine in each of the two dark blocks (@media prefers-color-scheme:dark and [data-theme="dark"]) — every token must keep a value in all three states. --action* is an alias of brand and follows on its own; --chart-*, the semantics and the whole ink ramp are untouched.

What stays warm

Cyag’s neutral ramp (#F8FAFC ground, #E2E8F0 rules, the slate ink scale) is not applied. Mezan’s canvas is warm paper a clear step darker than a card, on purpose: at a lighter ground a white panel and the canvas read as one flat sheet, which is exactly what a data screen must not look like. Swapping it is the full-identity option you set aside — say the word and this page gets a fourth specimen.
