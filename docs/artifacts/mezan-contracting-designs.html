01

What exists today

verified against code, not against docs

A project in Mezan is a header and nothing else: code, name, status, customer, manager, dates, one budget figure for the whole job, a site address, and an auto-created cost center PRJ-{code}. There is no BOQ, no certificate, no retention, no advance, no variation order, no subcontract, no cost rollup and no revenue recognition. ProjectsScreen.tsx is the only screen.

Already shipped — do not rebuild

  • Withholding tax (خصم وتحصيل) — posts on vouchers to account 2210, and reports/withholding-tax.ts reports it. Payable side only.
  • Four contracting account slots — contracting.revenue 4100, retention_receivable 1150, customer_advances 2160, late_penalty 5910.
  • The CONTRACT numbering sequence (CTR-) — defined, with zero consumers.
  • GL with balance and period enforcement, WAC inventory, purchasing RFQ→PO→GRN→invoice, landed cost, treasury (10 voucher types, 7-state cheques), the 26-action workflow gate, the audit trail.

Shipped and broken by the gap

  • The voucher type «دفعة مقدمة من عميل مقاولات» posts a liability to 2160 whose own subtitle says it is recovered من المستخلصات — a document that does not exist. The product records a liability it cannot discharge.
  • projects carries exactly two permissions, view and manage. One right covers creating the project, the BOQ, the variation order, the certificate and the subcontractor — no segregation of duties is expressible.
  • Retention exists receivable-only. Retention withheld from a subcontractor has nowhere to go and would silently drift inside AP.
  • PrintView.tsx cannot paginate. The one document this module exists to produce cannot be printed.

The blocker: cost has no way to reach a project

projectId appears in zero wire contracts. costCenterId appears in four, and reaches exactly one of ten posting paths — the smallest one. A project P&L built today renders nearly empty, and an empty P&L does not look like a bug; it looks like a quiet site.

02

The decision ledger

12 taken · each with its source and its reversal

The research recorded ten questions for the owner and two contested items, and resolved none of them on purpose. Every one is answered below. Where a decision is contested by named reviewers, the losing option is drawn, not just described.

D-01Is projectId a first-class dimension on every purchase, stock and treasury document — and is it one dimension or two?
→Two dimensions, mandatory, on all ten posting paths.

projectId plus a costCategory enum (مواد / عمالة / معدات / مقاول باطن / غير مباشرة). Not the cost center: a cost center cannot reach a goods receipt or a material issue, and hanging contracting off it forces customers to invent fake centers named PRJ-07-مواد, encoding meaning the code never rolls up. Both dimensions are written into the journal line and are immutable with it.

What reverses it: nothing, after the first posted entry. This is the one irreversible decision in the set, which is why it is first and why no screen work starts before it. 00-baseline.md · 5 reviewers, independently
D-02Is the مستخلص a document that generates a sales invoice, or the invoice itself with extra columns?
→Its own document. The invoice is generated from it, never the reverse.

Unanimous across every reviewer who bills, books or buys — and the buyer's words were that a certificate implemented as a sales-invoice variant is a walk-away. It is also now legally load-bearing: since July 2025 the invoice triggers VAT and the certificate is what the invoice must be traceable to. A certificate carries cumulative quantities, eleven deduction types and a two-stage approval that no invoice schema should ever learn.

What reverses it: nothing short of Egyptian practice changing. The invoice stays a thin derived document, so the AR, aging, ETA and allocation machinery is reused unchanged. 01-stakeholder-review.md · 05-egypt.md
D-03Does an approved certificate recognise revenue, or only bill it?
→Only bills it. Revenue is recognised cost-to-cost under EAS 48, as a separate monthly posting.

This is the panel's sharpest disagreement and the one place I overrode the majority. Six reviewers voted for certified value because it is simple and it ships. But the certified value is an output measure, and Egypt applies EAS 48 (الإيراد من العقود مع العملاء), under which billing is not revenue. On a certified-value basis every contract asset and contract liability is zero by construction, and the module cannot be audited. The decisive point is asymmetric risk: the ledger accounts must exist from day one because they cannot be retrofitted, while the recognition policy is a per-contract setting that costs one field.

What reverses it: a per-contract switch «الاعتراف عند الاعتماد» that recognises at certified value for tenants who do not need an audit opinion. Ships in the same release, defaulted off. 05-egypt.md · EAS 48, Decree 69/2019 — effective date UNVERIFIED

  • Contract asset / liability always zero

D-04Is retention one concept with a direction, or receivable-only?
→One concept, two directions. Add contracting.retention_payable now.

Shipping the receivable alone is the module's first asymmetry, and it gets more expensive later, not less — retention withheld from a subcontractor would be booked into AP where it silently drifts and is released on the wrong clock. The Arabic label is fixed at «المحتجزات الضمانية المستحقة لمقاولي الباطن»: same root noun as the customer side, opposite direction, so the pair can never drift into two different words.

What reverses it: nothing. It is one account slot and one sign. 01-stakeholder-review.md · 02-terminology.md
D-05Is contracting a vertical Mezan sells, or a ledger hole being closed?
→A sold vertical, with subcontractor certificates in release 1.

The owner ranked contracting above both POS and HR — higher ticket, stickier, and far fewer credible Arabic competitors. The pricing gap in the research is the tell: the owner guessed ~500 EGP/month, the actual contracting buyer said 1,500–2,000, and double that with subcontractor certificates and withholding in release 1. The buyer is the one holding the price. Seats-only, never per project or per BOQ line — a per-project axis is unforecastable and would not be signed.

What reverses it: a failed catalog pass against a real contractor's مستخلص. That pass is a prerequisite, not a follow-up. 01-stakeholder-review.md · commercial read
D-06Is the buyer the contractor's accountant or his site engineer?
→The accountant. The engineer imports from Excel.

The owner's own answer, and it settles a great deal of screen work: no mobile capture, no offline site app, no photo attachment flow in v1. What the engineer needs is one honest Excel import for the BOQ and one for the measurement sheet, both landing in a review grid the accountant approves. Design for a keyboard, a desk and a dusty spreadsheet — not for a phone on scaffolding.

What reverses it: field data-entry demand from real installs. The two-step material issue (D-11) is the seam where a site app would later attach. 01-stakeholder-review.md · Q6
D-07Does contracting VAT run at the standard rate with full input credit, or a reduced rate with input tax disallowed?
→Both, in parallel, keyed on contract date. Never a company-wide setting.

Law 157/2025 moved construction into the general VAT regime on 18 July 2025. Contracts signed — or carrying an approved certificate or e-invoice — before that date stay on the transitional basis of 14% on 36% of value (≈5.04%, engineered to preserve the old schedule-tax burden) with no input recovery. Every Egyptian contractor alive today has both kinds of contract on its books simultaneously, so a single tenant-wide VAT setting produces a wrong return.

What reverses it: the transitional regime aging out — which is why the basis is an effective-dated table row per contract, not a code branch. 05-egypt.md · Law 157/2025, MoF Decrees 417 & 418/2025
D-08When an approved variation order raises contract value after certificates were issued at the old value, does the retention ceiling recompute retrospectively or apply forward only?
→Forward only, with an optional cumulative cap.

Retrospective recomputation would require editing certificates that are already approved, printed and — in the public case — cleared for payment. The rate on each certificate is stamped at approval and never recalculated; a variation changes the base for certificates that come after it. The optional cap is a per-contract ceiling on cumulative retention as a percentage of contract value, applied when reached rather than backdated.

What reverses it: a customer contract that explicitly requires retrospective true-up — handled as a one-line adjustment on the next certificate, not as a rewrite. 01-stakeholder-review.md · Q8 · ceiling UNVERIFIED in primary text
D-09Does "approved" mean the consultant signed, or the client's finance department cleared it for payment?
→Two distinct states with two dates. Collections age from the second.

Those are weeks apart, and that gap is where collections go blind. معتمد من الاستشاري certifies the quantities; معتمد للصرف is the employer's finance clearance and starts the statutory payment clock — 60 days for works certificates under Law 182/2018 Art. 45, against 30 for supply and services. Late payment by a government entity carries compensation at the CBE credit-and-discount rate, which the module computes and almost no contractor ever invoices.

What reverses it: nothing; a single approval state cannot represent the gap. 05-egypt.md · Law 182/2018 Art. 45
D-10Is there a BOQ in v1?
→Yes, with Excel import. It is the spine, not a feature.

Product argued the captured catalog evidences one screen and no BOQ. But a certificate measures against something: without a BOQ there is nothing to hold cumulative quantity per item, cost cannot be compared to an item-level budget, and a variation cannot be told apart from base scope. The estimator's point closes it — if the tender's priced BOQ is not the project's BOQ, the estimate-versus-actual loop the whole module exists for never closes. The owner put Excel BOQ import second on the demo list.

What reverses it: nothing structural. A contractor with no BOQ uses a single-line BOQ, which is exactly product's "free description and an amount" as a degenerate case. 01-stakeholder-review.md · contested #1
D-11Is a site material issue a first-class document with a mandatory projectId, or a stock adjustment with the project in a note?
→First-class, and two-step: صرف by the storekeeper, استلام by a different person on site.

A project typed into a note makes "material cost by project" unqueryable, which is the question the module exists to answer. The two-step split is a control, not ceremony: it is the only thing that detects material that left the main store and never arrived. Material moving to a site store is an ordinary transfer at source WAC — valuation-neutral, no posting; stock stays inventory (1200) while it sits on site with a named custodian. A separate consumption document is the only stock→project-cost conversion.

What reverses it: nothing. Returnable materials (شدة وسقالات) are the exception and are tracked on a custody register with no valuation effect at all. 01-stakeholder-review.md · cost accountant + warehouse keeper
D-12When is WIP relieved to cost of contract revenue?
→At each recognition run, not at project close.

The research left this explicitly open. Relieving at close makes WIP a pot nobody can reconcile until handover — and on a two-year job that is two years of an unreconcilable control account. Relieving monthly, in step with the cost-to-cost recognition of D-03, makes WIP a month-end tieable balance: opening WIP + cost incurred − cost recognised = closing WIP, provable against the project sub-ledger every period.

What reverses it: choosing certified-value recognition per contract (D-03's switch), under which relief follows the certificate instead. Both paths relieve monthly; only the driver differs. 01-stakeholder-review.md · open question, decided here
Two decisions the research had already settled, restated so they are not relitigated: the real-estate developer is not this module (unit inventory, reservation and a 7-year instalment book share no structure with BOQ-and-certificate billing), and the Arabic glossary in 02-terminology.md is final — جدول الكميات not المقايسة, مستودع الموقع not مخزن, مناقصة never عطاء.

04

The مستخلص

cumulative snapshot · eleven deductions · two approvals

A certificate stores cumulative-to-date, never the period. Per BOQ line it holds previously certified quantity, this period, and cumulative — and the period value is a subtraction, not an input. A hard previousCertificateId and a strictly increasing serial per project, with no edit or void while a later certificate exists. Storing the period figure instead is how the same work gets billed twice.

The arithmetic

جملة الأعمال = الكمية × النسبة × الفئة
quantity × completion ratio × unit rate, per BOQ item, cumulative

نسبة الممارسة

The tender discount — a percentage bid off the priced BOQ, applied to every certificate as its own line, before deductions. No generic project module has this.

التشوينات

Material delivered and jointly inspected but not yet built in, certified in a separate column at a per-contract rate (conventionally 75%). When incorporated it is entered as a negative quantity in the current-period column.

One pile of rebar, three places, three values. As تشوينات at 75% on the certificate, as عهدة on the site foreman, and as executed work once consumed. A design that models only one of the three double-counts the other two.

Guarantees — the contractor's real ceiling

A contractor's guarantee limit at its bank, not its cash, is what caps how many jobs it can run at once. A register that reports guarantees outstanding by bank, by project and by expiry is reporting the customer's actual binding constraint — and not one of the fourteen Arabic-market products surveyed advertises it. Two things must be recorded, and generic ERPs record only one: the face value off balance sheet in memorandum accounts (نظامي مدين / نظامي دائن), and the cash margin on balance sheet as restricted cash.

06

Controls

what the server recomputes · who may act · what gets reported

Never accepted from the client

Each of these arrives in a request body somewhere in the codebase today. In this module every one is derived from posted documents:

  • cumulative executed quantity per BOQ item, and the previous certificate's cumulative
  • remaining contracted quantity, and whether a variation order raised it
  • retention held to date, and advance outstanding
  • a subcontract's cumulative certified value, and whether it exceeds the client certificate for the same line
  • project cost to date, and cost against budget
  • equipment hours billed
The rejection cases matter as much as the arithmetic. A certificate line whose quantity depends on a variation order approved after the work was executed must be refused, not quietly accepted.

Four rights, not one

projects.project.manage today covers creating the project, the BOQ, the variation order, the certificate and the subcontractor. It becomes:

Preparer ≠ approver is enforced, not advisory. And a subcontractor must carry a tax number and a bank account before any certificate for them can post.

07

Print

the document the module exists to produce

PrintView.tsx renders a single overflow-hidden column with one header and one totals block. There is no pagination, no repeated table header and no carried-forward subtotal, and pageSize is A4 | A5 with no orientation field — though the Electron print path already carries an unused landscape flag. A twelve-column BOQ grid in portrait either truncates Arabic descriptions or becomes unreadable, and truncation here is financial data.

What the renderer must gain

  • ما قبله — the carried-forward subtotal as the first row of every page after the first
  • يُرحل — the carried-to subtotal as the last row of every page before the last
  • the identity header repeated on every page, and «صفحة x من y»
  • the signature block on the final page only
  • a landscape option — wire the flag that already exists

A new totals block

PrintTotalsConfig knows subtotal, discount, tax and amount-in-words. It knows nothing about إجمالي العقد، المنفذ التراكمي، نسبة الإنجاز، المحتجزات، خصم الدفعة المقدمة or صافي المستحق — six fields that are the certificate's entire point.

Certificate print lines are templated strings with named placeholders in the resource file. Assembling a sentence at runtime from description + quantity + rate scrambles Arabic word order.
