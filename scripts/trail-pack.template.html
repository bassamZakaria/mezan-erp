Two rules that make a trail worth running

  • Check a pair, never a single figure. One plausible number hides everything; two that must agree cannot. Every trail names its pair in the closing line.
  • A status is not a posting. مدفوع, مؤكد and مُرحّل are claims. The claim is true when the ledger moved with it — assert the GL beside the flag, never the flag alone.

Reload before you tick

Take the steps in order, in the UI, and open the screens named under each one before ticking it. Reload the page first — reloading is what catches "works until you look again".

Ticks are remembered in this browser only. They never reach the repo, and clearing them loses nothing but your place.

What a trail is worth. A module scenario asks whether a screen does what it says. A trail asks whether the rest of the product agrees with what that screen just did.

That question found four defects in one afternoon — an invoice no screen can confirm, a collection that posted nothing, a supplier column showing the wrong field, and a purchase invoice that quietly drops VAT. None of them was visible to a screen-level test, because every screen involved answered 200 and looked right. Two were fixed by the D1–D37 sweep before this pack merged; the other two are D43 and D44.

Most steps here are marked verified because that sweep closed 37 defects. Where a step says fixed (F53) — check it has stayed fixed, the sentence after it is what used to happen: that is the regression the step exists to catch.
